Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Data you paste into the tools

The tools on Parsekit (such as the JSON Formatter, JSON to CSV, Merge JSON, JSON Diff, JSON Splitter and the other converters) run entirely in your browser. Whatever you paste into a tool is processed locally by JavaScript on the page and is never sent to, or stored on, any Parsekit server. The tools do not save your input in cookies, local storage or similar browser storage.

Cookies and advertising

This site does not currently show ads. In the future it may display ads served by Google AdSense. If that happens, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and this policy will be updated accordingly. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, at the Google Ads Policy page.

Analytics

Parsekit uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate traffic, such as which pages are visited and which tool buttons are used. Google Analytics may set cookies and collect information such as your approximate location, device and browser type. It does not receive anything you paste into the tools, and it is not used to identify visitors by name. You can learn more at How Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Third-party links

Pages on this site may link to third-party resources. Parsekit is not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Children's privacy

Parsekit is a general-audience developer tools site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site grows. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact
