Data you paste into the tools

Parsekit's tools (JSON Formatter, JSON to CSV, Merge JSON) run entirely in your browser. Whatever you paste into a tool is processed locally by JavaScript on the page and is never sent to, or stored on, any Parsekit server.

Cookies and advertising

This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, at Google's Ads Policy page.

Analytics

Parsekit may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic (for example, how many people visit a page), which does not identify individual visitors by name or paste content.

Third-party links

Pages on this site may link to third-party resources. Parsekit is not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Children's privacy

Parsekit is a general-audience developer tools site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site grows. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
